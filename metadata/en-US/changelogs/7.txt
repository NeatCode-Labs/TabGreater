Tokenized stocks in the pair search.

• "+ Add pair" and the widget setup have a Crypto | Stocks switch; Crypto, the default, no longer mixes in stock tokens
• Stocks finds the tokens for a ticker such as TSLA across the exchanges, Kraken's included, with popular tickers one tap away
• These are tokens on crypto exchanges, not stock-exchange quotes: prices can differ from the share price, and some trade only on weekdays
• The widget setup hides its preview while you search
